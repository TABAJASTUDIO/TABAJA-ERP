TABAJA ERP — ALPHA 10 TESTING

Main changes:
1. Multiple stock-item lines in one Purchase Voucher.
2. After Rate, Enter completes the line and opens a new blank item line.
3. End of List is selectable in the Stock Item list and moves to Narration.
4. Escape while editing cancels only the current uncommitted edit.
5. Escape without a changed value moves to the previous field without deleting it.
6. Escape from Narration returns to the last item's Rate while preserving Item, Quantity and Rate.
7. A fresh voucher is still empty after leaving and entering again.

Suggested test:
- Enter two or three items.
- Choose End of List.
- Type Narration, press Esc once to cancel its text, then Esc again to return to last Rate.
- Continue Esc through Quantity and Item and confirm values remain.
