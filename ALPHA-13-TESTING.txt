TABAJA ERP - ALPHA 13 TESTING

FIX 1 - ALTER COMPANY
1. Open K: Company > Alter.
2. Change the company name.
3. Save with Ctrl+A or the Alter Company button.
4. Confirm the new name appears immediately on Gateway and remains after refresh.

FIX 2 - RETURN FROM NARRATION TO LAST ITEM
1. Enter two stock items and go to Narration.
2. If Narration has unconfirmed text, first Esc cancels that text.
3. Press Esc again.
4. Focus must return to the last saved item and open List of Stock Items with End of List selected.
5. Press Enter on End of List to remove that last item.
6. Totals must update and focus returns to Narration.

No visual layout dimensions were changed from Alpha 12.
