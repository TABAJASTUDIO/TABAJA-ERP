Stock Item Creation keyboard flow:
1. Open Create from List of Stock Items.
2. Enter moves: Name -> Unit -> Weight per Unit -> Opening Quantity.
3. Press Enter on Opening Quantity.
4. “Accept?” appears with Yes selected.
5. Press Enter to save and return to the voucher.
6. On Accept?, arrows switch between Yes and No.
7. Esc on Accept? returns to Opening Quantity without closing creation.
8. Ctrl+A still saves immediately from any field.
