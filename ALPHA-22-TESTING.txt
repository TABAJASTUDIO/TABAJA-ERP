TABAJA ERP ALPHA 22 — VOUCHER REVERSE CELL PATH

Test only this sequence:
1. Enter at least two stock items.
2. Go to End of List, then Narration.
3. Press Esc: focus must return to Amount of the last item.
4. Press Esc: focus must return to Rate.
5. Press Esc: focus must return to Quantity.
6. Press Esc: focus must return to Item name.
7. Press Backspace while on Item: remove one character at a time, not the full name.
8. At no point may focus jump to Purchase Ledger.
