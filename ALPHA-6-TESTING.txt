Main change: Central Keyboard Engine

Test checklist:
1. Gateway: Arrow Up/Down, Enter, C/A/H/V/D/Q hotkeys.
2. Esc on Gateway opens Quit; Esc again selects No and restores focus.
3. Master menus: Arrow Up/Down, Enter, Esc.

7. Ctrl+A inside forms saves/submits.

No stored company data is removed; browser localStorage remains compatible.
